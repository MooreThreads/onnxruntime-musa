# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, professional, and harassment-free
community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus technical discussion on evidence, reproducible behavior, and concrete
  tradeoffs.
- Accept feedback gracefully and keep review discussions on topic.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, and disruptive
  behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, abusive language, sustained
disruption of project discussions, public disclosure of private information, or
any other conduct that maintainers determine is inappropriate for the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or
restrict participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.

To report conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through the
repository's private maintainer channel or the official Moore Threads project
contact path.
